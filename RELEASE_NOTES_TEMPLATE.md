## About the game

NEON RUSH is an offline, futuristic 2D Android endless runner built with Kotlin, LibGDX, and Gradle. Players move across three lanes, avoid different obstacles, collect energy orbs, and compete against their own high scores.

## Gameplay highlights

- Three-lane movement with touch controls and swipe gestures
- Jump and slide actions for different obstacle types
- Energy orb collection, scoring, and progressive difficulty
- Pause, resume, retry, saved best score, and sound preferences
- Neon-inspired procedural visuals

## Installation

1. Download the attached APK from this Release's **Assets** section.
2. Open the downloaded file on a compatible Android device.
3. Follow Android's confirmation to install it if you trust the source.
4. Launch NEON RUSH and try a run.

This preview is not distributed through Google Play. It is not a signed production release.

## Known limitations

- Offline single-player only; no Firebase or multiplayer
- Compatibility and performance vary by device
- The fixed 480 × 800 virtual viewport may letterbox on taller phones
- Gameplay balance, audio transitions, and Android lifecycle need further real-device testing
